Gardening

Images from Unsplash

Gardening is a beautiful hobby. You get seeds, plant them, water them and plants grow. This can include flowers, vegetables, trees, bushes, cacti, etc. Someone who likes to garden is commonly called a “green thumb”. Gardens are beautiful, but sustaining them is hard work. One has to deal with the heat, sweat, thorns, dirt, and even the time it takes to grow plants. Some plants grow quicker than others. Some plants take years to grow. Many students garden for class assignments, such as in elementary school or high school. Gardens can be big and professional or small and humble. Not all gardens look like the ones in the photos here. Some can be a simple plant in a pot. Some gardeners even enroll in gardening contests. An example is a pumpkin growing contest, where whoever grows the largest pumpkin wins. This is usually an Autumn event. Gardens look different throughout the world. A garden in the US looks very different from one in Japan, for example. You can learn about gardening through gardening books, gardening videos, and even trying gardening yourself.
